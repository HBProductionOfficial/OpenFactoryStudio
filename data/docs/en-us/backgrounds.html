Backgrounds

The Backgrounds editor shows the background elements of a stage, screenpack or storyboard the way the engine draws them. That covers normal, animated, parallax and dummy elements, with their start, delta, velocity, tiling, transparency, window and sine motion. You can move the camera to scroll the view.

  • In the left panel you edit the current element. You can add, delete, duplicate and reorder elements.
  • Play runs the background with its controllers. The right panel lists the controllers and their parameters.
  • Every change goes into the definitions file as text, and you can edit that text too.
