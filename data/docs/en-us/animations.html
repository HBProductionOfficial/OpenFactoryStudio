Animations

The Animations editor shows the current frame of an animation (an action in the AIR file) with its sprite, offset, flips, scale and angle. Under it is a timeline with the animation's frames.

Frames

  • In the left panel you edit the animation's number and name, and the frame's sprite, offset, time, flips, transparency, loop start, scale, angle and interpolation. Drag the sprite to change its offset.
  • Animations menu: add, delete and duplicate animations and frames, apply a frame's values to the frames after it, the alignment tool, and optimize.
  • Play runs the animation at 60 ticks a second. It loops back to the loop start.
  • An onion skin shows a frame from another animation under the current one.
  • Edit code shows the whole file as text. The visual editor and the text always stay in sync.
  • Save as animated GIF exports the animation.

Contact boxes

You draw attack (Clsn1) and collision (Clsn2) boxes with the box tools. You can select, move and resize them, and nudge them with the arrow keys. You can also copy them from the previous frame, or copy them to every frame that uses the same sprite. Default boxes are used on frames that have no boxes of their own. View > Attack box and Collision box show or hide them.
