Open Factory Studio reference

Open Factory Studio edits the files that make up a M.U.G.E.N game: characters, stages, screenpacks (motifs), fight screens, storyboards, fonts and select screens. Each open project gets its own tab. To switch between its editors, use the buttons on the main toolbar or the editor menus.

  • Sprites: sprite files, palettes and the image editor.
  • Animations: frames, contact boxes, playback and GIF export.
  • Backgrounds: stage and screen backgrounds and their controllers.
  • Sounds: sound files, editing the waveform, and converting sounds.

These pages are only about Open Factory Studio. The M.U.G.E.N engine and its data belong to their authors, and Open Factory Studio doesn't come with them.
