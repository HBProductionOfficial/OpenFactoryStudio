Sounds

The Sounds editor shows the sounds in an SND file. For the current sound you see its waveform with a time ruler in ticks, its group and number, its format and its length.

  • Select a range with the mouse or in the panel. Then you can remove it, fill it with silence, fade it in or out, cut, copy and paste. These edits wait until you click Save changes or Discard changes.
  • Play, play looping, pause and stop. Playback starts from the cursor.
  • Add sounds from WAV, OGG, FLAC and MP3 files. Save a sound as WAV, OGG or FLAC. Resample sounds to a different number of channels, sample rate or bit depth.
  • You can rename or delete groups. You can also export sound lists for SndMaker and import them back.
