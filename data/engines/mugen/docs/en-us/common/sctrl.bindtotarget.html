BindToTarget

Keeps the player at a point relative to one of its targets.

Parameters

timeinteger expressionTicks the binding lasts. Default 1.
IDinteger expressionTarget ID to bind to; -1 picks any target. Default -1.
pos2 expressions, then Foot, Mid, HeadX and y offset, optionally followed by the anchor on the target: Foot (axis, the default), Mid or Head (from the target size constants mid.pos and head.pos).

Example

[State 900, Ride]
type = BindToTarget
trigger1 = 1
pos = 0, -10, Head
