EnvShake

Shakes the screen up and down.

Parameters

timeinteger expression, requiredTicks the shake lasts.
freqfloat expressionSpeed of the shake, 0 (slow) to 180 (fast). Default 60.
amplinteger expressionDistance of the shake; negative values shake downwards first. The default depends on the coordinate space (-4 in 320x240).
phasefloat expressionPhase offset. Defaults to 0, or 90 when freq is 90 or more.

Example

[State 1100, Impact]
type = EnvShake
trigger1 = AnimElem = 4
time = 15
ampl = -6
