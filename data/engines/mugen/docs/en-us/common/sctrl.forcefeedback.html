ForceFeedback

Makes a force feedback controller vibrate. Not implemented in 1.0.

Parameters

waveformone of: Sine, Square, SineSquare, OffSine (large motor), Square (small motor), SineSquare (both) or Off.
timeintegerDuration in ticks. Default 60.
freqinteger and 3 numbersStart value (0-255) and the coefficients of t, t squared and t cubed. Ignored by the engine.
amplinteger and 3 numbersStart strength (0-255) and the coefficients of t, t squared and t cubed.
selfinteger1 vibrates the own pad, 0 the opponent pad. Default 1.

Notes

  • Unlike other controllers, its parameters must be constants, not expressions.

Example

[State 5000, Rumble]
type = ForceFeedback
trigger1 = Time = 0
waveform = Square
time = 10
