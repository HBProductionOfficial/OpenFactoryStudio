Pause

Freezes the game: players and background stop updating.

Parameters

timeinteger expression, requiredTicks to pause.
endcmdbuftimeinteger expression (WinMUGEN and later)Ticks at the end of the pause during which frozen players can buffer commands. Default 0.
movetimeinteger expressionTicks at the start during which the player may still move (and hit). Default 0.
pausebgexpression, 0 or 1 (WinMUGEN and later)0 keeps the background moving during the pause. Default 1.

Notes

  • A new Pause cancels the previous one. A Pause during a SuperPause waits for it to end.

Example

[State 3000, Freeze]
type = Pause
trigger1 = Time = 0
time = 20
movetime = 20
