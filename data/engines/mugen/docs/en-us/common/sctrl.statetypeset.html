StateTypeSet

Changes the state type, move type and physics without leaving the state.

Parameters

statetypeone of: S, C, A, LS standing, C crouching, A air or L lying.
movetypeone of: I, A, HI idle, A attacking or H being hit.
physicsone of: S, C, A, NS, C, A or N (none).

Example

[State 1100, Take off]
type = StateTypeSet
trigger1 = AnimElem = 3
statetype = A
physics = A
