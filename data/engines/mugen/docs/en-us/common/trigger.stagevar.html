StageVar

Old-style trigger: compares a property of the current stage with a string.

Available from M.U.G.E.N 1.0.

Format

StageVar(name) = "text"
StageVar(name) != "text"

Arguments

nameinfo.name, info.displayname or info.author.

Returns

boolean (1 or 0)

Example

trigger1 = StageVar(info.name) = "Training Room"
