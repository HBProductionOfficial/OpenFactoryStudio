GameMakeAnim

Old way to play a fightfx animation such as a spark. Superseded by Explod.

Deprecated since 1.0.

Parameters

valueinteger expressionFightfx animation number. Default 0.
underexpression, 0 or 11 draws it behind the players.
pos2 float expressionsOffset from the player axis.
randominteger expressionRange of a random displacement in x and y.

Example

[State 1000, Old spark]
type = GameMakeAnim
trigger1 = Time = 5
value = 2
pos = 30, -50
