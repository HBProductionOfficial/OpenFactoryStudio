SndPan

Moves a sound that is playing in the stereo field; trigger it every tick to follow the player.

Parameters

channelinteger expression, requiredChannel of the sound.
paninteger expressionOffset from the player (see PlaySnd).
abspaninteger expressionOffset from the screen centre. Use pan or abspan, not both.

Example

[State 1000, Follow sound]
type = SndPan
trigger1 = 1
channel = 3
pan = 0
