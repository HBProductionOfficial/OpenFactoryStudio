TargetBind

Holds the targets at a point relative to the player axis.

Parameters

timeinteger expressionTicks the binding lasts. Default 1.
IDinteger expressionOnly targets hit with this HitDef id; -1 for all.
pos2 float expressionsX and y offset from the player axis.

Example

[State 810, Hold]
type = TargetBind
trigger1 = AnimElem = 1
pos = 30, -10
