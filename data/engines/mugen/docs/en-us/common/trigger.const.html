Const

Returns one of the constants of the player (the [Data], [Size], [Velocity] and [Movement] groups).

Format

Const(name)

Arguments

nameA name such as data.life, size.height, velocity.walk.fwd.x or movement.yaccel.

Returns

int or float, depending on the constant

Example

trigger1 = Pos Y > Const(movement.air.gethit.groundlevel)
