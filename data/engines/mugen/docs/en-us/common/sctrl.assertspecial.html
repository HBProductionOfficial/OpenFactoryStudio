AssertSpecial

Raises up to three engine flags. Flags only last one tick, so the controller has to run every tick they are needed.

Parameters

flagone of: intro, invisible, roundnotover, nobardisplay, noBG, noFG, nostandguard, nocrouchguard, noairguard, noautoturn, nojugglecheck, nokosnd, nokoslow, noshadow, globalnoshadow, nomusic, nowalk, timerfreeze, unguardable, requiredFirst flag to raise (see the list below).
flag2one of: intro, invisible, roundnotover, nobardisplay, noBG, noFG, nostandguard, nocrouchguard, noairguard, noautoturn, nojugglecheck, nokosnd, nokoslow, noshadow, globalnoshadow, nomusic, nowalk, timerfreeze, unguardableOptional second flag.
flag3one of: intro, invisible, roundnotover, nobardisplay, noBG, noFG, nostandguard, nocrouchguard, noairguard, noautoturn, nojugglecheck, nokosnd, nokoslow, noshadow, globalnoshadow, nomusic, nowalk, timerfreeze, unguardableOptional third flag.

Notes

  • Flags: Intro (intro pose is playing), Invisible (player not drawn), RoundNotOver (win pose still playing), NoBarDisplay (hide lifebars), NoBG / NoFG (hide the stage background / foreground), NoStandGuard, NoCrouchGuard, NoAirGuard, NoAutoTurn, NoJuggleCheck, NoKOSnd (no KO cry), NoKOSlow (no slow motion at the end of the round), NoShadow, GlobalNoShadow (no shadows for anyone), NoMusic (pause the music), NoWalk, TimerFreeze (round timer stops), Unguardable (the player's attacks cannot be guarded).

Example

[State 191, Intro]
type = AssertSpecial
trigger1 = 1
flag = Intro
flag2 = NoBarDisplay
