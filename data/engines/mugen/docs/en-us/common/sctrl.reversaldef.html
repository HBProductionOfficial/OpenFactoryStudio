ReversalDef

Counters attacks: when an attack box of the opponent touches an attack box of the player while the ReversalDef is active, the attack is reversed.

Parameters

reversal.attrhit attribute, requiredHit attribute string of the attacks that can be reversed.
pausetime2 integer expressionsTicks P1 freezes, then ticks P2 shakes, on reversal.
sparknointeger expression, S/F prefix allowedSpark animation (S prefix for the player AIR file).
sparkxy2 integer expressionsOffset added to the sparkxy of the reversed HitDef.
hitsound2 integer expressions, S/F prefix allowedSound played on reversal (S prefix for the player sounds).
p1statenointeger expressionState the player goes to on reversal.
p2statenointeger expressionState the opponent goes to on reversal.

Notes

  • MoveHit tells whether the reversal succeeded.

Example

[State 1600, Parry]
type = ReversalDef
trigger1 = Time = 0
reversal.attr = SA, NA, SA
pausetime = 12, 12
p1stateno = 1610
