HitDef

Defines a single hit. When an attack box (Clsn1) of the player touches a hurt box (Clsn2) of an opponent while the HitDef is active, the hit is applied. Each hit of a multi-hit move needs its own HitDef.

Parameters

attrhit attribute, requiredHit attribute: the state type of the attack (S, C or A) and its class, two letters: N, S or H (normal, special, hyper) followed by A, T or P (attack, throw, projectile). Example: S, NA.
hitflaghit flagsStates in which the opponent can be hit: H high, L low, A air, M (= HL), F falling, D lying down, plus + (only opponents already being hit) or - (only opponents not being hit). Default MAF.
guardflagguard flagsHow the attack can be guarded: H, L, A or M. Empty (default) means it cannot be guarded.
affectteamone of: E, B, FWho can be hit: E enemies (default), B both teams, F own team.
animtypeone of: Light, Medium, Hard, Back, Up, DiagUpGet-hit animation of the opponent: Light (default), Medium, Hard, Back (knocked off the feet), Up or DiagUp.
air.animtypeone of: Light, Medium, Hard, Back, Up, DiagUpAnimation type used when the opponent is in the air. Defaults to animtype.
fall.animtypeone of: Light, Medium, Hard, Back, Up, DiagUpAnimation type used when the opponent is falling. Defaults to Up when air.animtype is Up, Back otherwise.
priorityexpression, then Hit, Miss, DodgePriority from 1 to 7 (default 4), optionally followed by Hit, Miss or Dodge, which decides what happens when both players hit each other at the same priority.
damage2 integer expressionsDamage on hit, then damage when guarded. Default 0,0.
pausetime2 integer expressionsTicks P1 freezes, then ticks P2 shakes, when the hit connects. Default 0,0.
guard.pausetime2 integer expressionsThe same when the attack is guarded. Defaults to pausetime.
sparknointeger expression, S/F prefix allowedHit spark animation. Prefix with S to use the player AIR file. Defaults to the [Data] value.
guard.sparknointeger expression, S/F prefix allowedSpark shown when guarded, with the same S prefix rule.
sparkxy2 integer expressionsSpark position: x from the front edge of P2 (negative goes inside), y relative to P1.
hitsound2 integer expressions, S/F prefix allowedSound group and number played on hit, from the common sound file; prefix S for the player sounds.
guardsound2 integer expressions, S/F prefix allowedSound played when guarded, with the same prefix rule.
ground.typeone of: High, Low, Trip, NoneHow a grounded opponent reacts: High (head snaps back), Low (stomach), Trip (swept off the feet, use with fall) or None (no reaction besides the pause). Default High.
air.typeone of: High, Low, Trip, NoneSame for an airborne opponent. Defaults to ground.type.
ground.slidetimeinteger expressionTicks the grounded opponent slides back after the shake.
guard.slidetimeinteger expressionSlide ticks when guarded. Defaults to guard.hittime.
ground.hittimeinteger expressionTicks a grounded opponent stays in the hit state; the key value for combos.
guard.hittimeinteger expressionHit time when guarded. Defaults to ground.hittime.
air.hittimeinteger expressionTicks before an opponent hit in the air can guard again (when not falling). Default 20.
guard.ctrltimeinteger expressionTicks before a guarding opponent regains control. Defaults to guard.slidetime.
guard.distinteger expressionDistance inside which the opponent starts guarding. Defaults to the [Size] value.
yaccelfloat expressionDownward acceleration given to the opponent. Default .35 in 320x240 space.
ground.velocity2 float expressionsX and y velocity of a grounded opponent after the hit; a non-zero y launches it.
guard.velocityfloat expressionX velocity of a guarding opponent. Defaults to the x of ground.velocity.
air.velocity2 float expressionsX and y velocity of an opponent hit in the air.
airguard.velocity2 float expressionsVelocity of an opponent guarding in the air. Defaults to 1.5 times the x and half the y of air.velocity.
ground.cornerpush.velofffloat expressionExtra push-back for P1 when hitting a grounded opponent in the corner.
air.cornerpush.velofffloat expressionCorner push-back when hitting an airborne opponent.
down.cornerpush.velofffloat expressionCorner push-back when hitting a lying opponent.
guard.cornerpush.velofffloat expressionCorner push-back when the attack is guarded.
airguard.cornerpush.velofffloat expressionCorner push-back when the attack is guarded in the air.
airguard.ctrltimeinteger expressionTicks before an opponent guarding in the air regains control.
air.juggleinteger expressionExtra juggle points the hit needs (not the StateDef juggle). Mostly for projectiles.
mindist2 integer expressionsClosest x, y position of P2 relative to P1 after the hit.
maxdist2 integer expressionsFarthest x, y position of P2 relative to P1 after the hit.
snap2 integer expressionsMoves P2 to this x, y position relative to P1 on hit. TargetBind is usually better.
p1sprpriorityinteger expression (WinMUGEN and later)Drawing priority of P1 after contact. Default 1.
p2sprpriorityinteger expression (WinMUGEN and later)Drawing priority of P2 after contact. Default 0.
sprpriorityinteger expression (deprecated since WinMUGEN)Old name of p1sprpriority.
p1facinginteger expression-1 turns P1 around when the hit connects.
p1getp2facinginteger expression1 makes P1 face the same way as P2, -1 the opposite way. Takes precedence over p1facing.
p2facinginteger expression1 makes P2 face the same way as P1, -1 the opposite way.
p1statenointeger expressionState P1 changes to when the hit connects (it must be an attack state). -1 = no change.
p2statenointeger expressionState P2 changes to; P2 then runs P1's states (a custom state), mainly for throws. -1 = no change.
p2getp1stateexpression, 0 or 10 keeps P2 in its own state data even when p2stateno is used.
forcestandexpression, 0 or 11 makes a crouching opponent stand. Defaults to 1 when ground.velocity has a y component.
fallexpression, 0 or 11 makes the opponent fall down without recovering control in the air.
fall.xvelocityfloat expressionX velocity of the bounce when the falling opponent hits the ground.
fall.yvelocityfloat expressionY velocity of that bounce. Default -4.5 in 320x240 space.
fall.recoverexpression, 0 or 10 prevents the opponent from recovering from the fall.
fall.recovertimeinteger expressionTicks before the opponent may recover. Default 4.
fall.damageinteger expressionDamage dealt when the falling opponent lands.
air.fallexpression, 0 or 11 makes an opponent hit in the air fall. Defaults to fall.
forcenofallexpression, 0 or 11 takes the opponent out of a fall. Ignored when fall is 1.
down.velocity2 float expressionsVelocity of an opponent hit while lying down. Defaults to air.velocity.
down.hittimeinteger expressionSlide ticks of an opponent hit while lying down (when not launched).
down.bounceexpression, 0 or 11 makes an opponent hit while lying down bounce once using the fall velocities.
idinteger expressionTarget ID given to opponents hit by this HitDef; used by target redirection and target controllers. 0 (default) means none.
chainIDinteger expressionThe hit only connects if P2 was last hit by a HitDef of this player with this id.
nochainID2 integer expressionsUp to two ids after which this hit cannot connect.
hitonceexpression, 0 or 11 hits a single opponent and drops other targets. Default 1 for throws, 0 otherwise.
killexpression, 0 or 10 prevents the hit from KO-ing the opponent.
guard.killexpression, 0 or 10 prevents guard damage from KO-ing the opponent.
fall.killexpression, 0 or 10 prevents fall damage from KO-ing the opponent.
numhitsinteger expressionHits added to the combo counter. Default 1.
getpower2 integer expressionsPower P1 gains on hit, then when guarded. Defaults depend on the damage and mugen.cfg.
givepower2 integer expressionsPower P2 gains on hit, then when guarded.
palfx.timeinteger expressionTicks of a palette effect on P2 when hit. Default 0 (none).
palfx.mul3 integer expressionsPalFX mul for that effect.
palfx.add3 integer expressionsPalFX add for that effect.
palfx.sinadd4 integer expressionsPalFX sinadd for that effect.
palfx.invertallexpression, 0 or 1PalFX invertall for that effect.
palfx.colorinteger expressionPalFX color for that effect.
envshake.timeinteger expressionTicks of a screen shake when the hit connects.
envshake.freqfloat expressionEnvShake freq of that shake.
envshake.amplinteger expressionEnvShake ampl of that shake.
envshake.phasefloat expressionEnvShake phase of that shake.
fall.envshake.timeinteger expressionTicks of a screen shake when the falling opponent lands.
fall.envshake.freqfloat expressionFrequency of that shake.
fall.envshake.amplinteger expressionAmplitude of that shake.
fall.envshake.phasefloat expressionPhase of that shake.

Notes

  • A HitDef stays active until it hits, the state changes (see hitdefpersist) or another HitDef replaces it.
  • Its behaviour is undefined in [Statedef -2] while the player is in a custom state.

Example

[State 210, Kick]
type = HitDef
trigger1 = Time = 0
attr = S, NA
damage = 35, 5
animtype = Medium
guardflag = MA
pausetime = 10, 10
sparkxy = -12, -55
hitsound = 5, 1
guardsound = 6, 0
ground.slidetime = 10
ground.hittime = 14
ground.velocity = -5
air.velocity = -3, -4
