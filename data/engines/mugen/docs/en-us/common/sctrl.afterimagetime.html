AfterImageTime

Changes the remaining duration of the afterimage trail that is currently running.

Parameters

timeinteger expressionNew duration in ticks; 0 ends the trail.
valueinteger expressionSame as time (alternative name).

Notes

  • Has no effect when no afterimage is active.
  • With a timegap other than 1 in the AfterImage controller, the stored frames may jump.

Example

[State 1000, End trail]
type = AfterImageTime
trigger1 = AnimTime = 0
time = 0
