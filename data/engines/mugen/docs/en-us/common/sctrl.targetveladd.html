TargetVelAdd

Adds to the velocity of the targets (positive x towards their front, positive y down).

Parameters

xfloat expressionX velocity to add.
yfloat expressionY velocity to add.
IDinteger expressionOnly targets with this id; -1 for all.

Example

[State 821, Gravity on victim]
type = TargetVelAdd
trigger1 = 1
y = 0.4
