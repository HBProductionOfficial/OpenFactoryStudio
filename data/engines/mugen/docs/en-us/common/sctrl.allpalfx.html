AllPalFX

Applies a palette effect to everything on screen: every player, helper and explod, the stage and the lifebars.

Parameters

timeinteger expressionHow many ticks the effect lasts. -1 keeps it until another palette effect replaces it; 0 cancels a running effect.
add3 integer expressionsValues added to the red, green and blue components of every colour (default 0,0,0).
mul3 integer expressionsMultipliers applied after the addition, where 256 leaves a component unchanged (default 256,256,256).
sinadd4 integer expressionsRed, green and blue amplitudes of an extra sine-wave addition, followed by its period in ticks.
invertallexpression, 0 or 1When non-zero, colours are inverted (a photographic negative) before add and mul are applied.
colorinteger expressionColour saturation from 0 (greyscale) to 256 (unchanged), applied first. Default 256.

Notes

  • Takes the same parameters as PalFX. Explods are affected even if they use their own palette.

Example

[State 3000, Flash]
type = AllPalFX
trigger1 = Time = 0
time = 6
add = 128, 128, 128
