AngleDraw

Draws the player rotated around its axis for the current tick, optionally scaled.

Parameters

valuefloat expressionAngle in degrees. When omitted, the angle set with AngleSet/AngleAdd/AngleMul is used.
scale2 float expressionsHorizontal and vertical drawing scale.

Notes

  • A positive angle turns counter-clockwise when the player faces right.
  • Only the picture changes: collision boxes keep their unrotated shape.
  • Must be triggered every tick the rotation should be visible.

Example

[State 1200, Draw]
type = AngleDraw
trigger1 = 1
scale = 1.2, 1.2
