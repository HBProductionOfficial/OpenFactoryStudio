Zoom

Zooms the camera. This 1.1 controller is not covered by the official documentation; the parameters below are the ones other engines read.

Available from M.U.G.E.N 1.1.

Parameters

pos2 float expressionsPoint to zoom on.
scalefloat expressionZoom factor.
lagfloat expressionDelay of the camera.
cameraboundexpression, 0 or 11 keeps the view inside the camera bounds.
timeinteger expressionTicks it lasts.
stageboundexpression, 0 or 11 keeps the view inside the stage.

Example

[State 3000, Close up]
type = Zoom
trigger1 = Time < 30
scale = 1.5
