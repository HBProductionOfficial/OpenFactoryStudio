TargetLifeAdd

Adds to the life of the targets, scaled by their defence unless absolute is set.

Parameters

valueinteger expression, requiredLife to add (negative to damage).
IDinteger expressionOnly targets with this id; -1 for all.
killexpression, 0 or 10 never takes life below 1.
absoluteexpression, 0 or 11 ignores attack and defence multipliers.

Example

[State 810, Slam]
type = TargetLifeAdd
trigger1 = AnimElem = 4
value = -80
