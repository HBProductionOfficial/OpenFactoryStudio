HitVelSet

Old controller: copies the get-hit velocity into the chosen components of the player velocity.

Deprecated since 1.0.

Parameters

xexpression, 0 or 1Non-zero copies the x component.
yexpression, 0 or 1Non-zero copies the y component.

Example

[State 5001, Old style]
type = HitVelSet
trigger1 = Time = 0
x = 1
