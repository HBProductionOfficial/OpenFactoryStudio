LifeAdd

Adds to the life of the player, scaled by its defence multiplier unless absolute is set.

Parameters

valueinteger expression, requiredLife to add (negative to remove).
killexpression, 0 or 10 never takes life below 1. Default 1.
absoluteexpression, 0 or 11 adds exactly value, ignoring the defence multiplier.

Example

[State 1400, Heal]
type = LifeAdd
trigger1 = Time % 10 = 0
value = 5
