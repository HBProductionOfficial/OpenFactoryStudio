RemapPal

Draws every sprite that uses one palette with another palette of the player.

Parameters

source2 integer expressionsGroup and item of the palette to replace. -1 remaps every indexed sprite.
dest2 integer expressionsGroup and item of the palette to use. -1 removes the mapping of source.

Notes

  • Mappings do not chain: mapping A to B and B to C does not map A to C.
  • In 1.1 a player can hold up to 8 mappings at once.

Example

[State 1500, Alternate colours]
type = RemapPal
trigger1 = Time = 0
source = 1, 1
dest = 1, 4
