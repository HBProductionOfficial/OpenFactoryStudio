DefenceMulSet

Sets the defence multiplier: every amount of damage the player takes is scaled by it.

Parameters

valuefloat expression, requiredMultiplier, 1 = normal damage, 0.5 = half damage.

Notes

  • LifeAdd ignores this multiplier.

Example

[State -2, Armour]
type = DefenceMulSet
trigger1 = 1
value = 0.75
