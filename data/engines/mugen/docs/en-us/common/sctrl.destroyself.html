DestroySelf

Removes the helper that executes it. Normal players ignore it.

Parameters

recursiveexpression, 0 or 1 (1.1 and later)When 1, helpers spawned by this helper are removed too.
removeexplodsexpression, 0 or 1 (1.1 and later)When 1, the explods of the helper (and of its descendants with recursive) are removed too.

Notes

  • Nothing after it in the state is executed.
  • Players and explods bound to the helper are released; explods left behind keep playing.

Example

[State 6000, Gone]
type = DestroySelf
trigger1 = AnimTime = 0
